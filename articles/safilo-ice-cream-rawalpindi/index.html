With its long cherished status among local residents and tourists alike, when one desires a refreshing and sweet indulgence, look no further than Safilo Ice Cream in Rawalpindi. Located in the heart of Rawalpindi's busy streets, this ice cream parlor offers more than just scoops of ice cream — it provides a broad menu catering to a wide range of tastes.

A Paradise of Ice Cream Flavors

At Safilo Ice Cream, customers will find a great variety of flavors. The menu includes the rich and nutty almond roasted flavor as well as the traditional kulfi, which dates back to South Asian heritage. For those wanting something fruity, there's mango or pineapple, while vanilla and chocolate remain favorites for their creamy, comforting sweetness.

Pista and coconut are also unique offerings that add to the charm of Safilo's menu, while the Tutti Frutti flavor is a delightful mix that surprises palates with every bite. Safilo's commitment to quality ensures each flavor is crafted using the finest ingredients.

Sizes and Pricing: Something for Everyone

  • Small Cup: Rs. 260 — affordable, suitable for a small treat.
  • Large Cup (3 scoops): Rs. 380.
  • Half Liter Pack: Rs. 650 — for sharing or indulging at home.
  • Full Liter Pack: Rs. 1280.

Beyond the Scoop: Juices and Shakes

Safilo's frozen desserts extend to a variety of fresh juices and shakes. There are about 10 different juice flavors, including pineapple, strawberry, and grapefruit, delivering natural sweetness — whether you want something tangy like orange or sweet like the house special.

For something creamier, the fresh shakes menu includes mango, banana, peach, and apple, along with more adventurous options like papaya or cheeko shakes, and Oreo fudge for chocolate lovers. Each shake is made thick and creamy — satisfying and cooling at once.

Lassi: A Traditional Delight

Lassi is a must-try — a traditional yogurt-based beverage that makes the place unique with its refreshing, wholesome taste. Safilo offers Mango Lassi, Strawberry Lassi, Sweet Lassi, and Saltish Lassi — thicker or thinner, sweet or salty, fruit-flavored or plain.

Hot Beverages and Soups

Apart from cold treats, Safilo Ice Cream also makes hot drinks and soups. Hot coffee and cold milk cost just Rs. 240, and a Hot & Sour Soup at Rs. 310 adds some savory variety to the menu.

Conclusion: A Destination for All Tastes

Rawalpindi has Safilo — not an ordinary ice cream shop, but a destination for delightful tastes. Whatever you're looking for, whether a rich ice-cream scoop, a cool juice, a creamy shake, or a lassi, Safilo has something to suit your taste, popular with both natives and visitors alike.
