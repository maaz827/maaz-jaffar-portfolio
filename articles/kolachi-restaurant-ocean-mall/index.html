Kolachi Restaurant Ocean Mall in Karachi can be said to be one of the best places to eat a meal you'll never forget. Located in Ocean Mall, one of Karachi's focal points for shopping and leisure, Kolachi is more than a restaurant — it promises and provides not only great and tasty food, but also a lovely view.

Location and Ambiance

Set on the higher storey of Ocean Mall, Kolachi has exceptional views of Karachi's skyline and the coast. The overall dress code is elegant with some traditional touches, perfect for family dinners, dates, or business meetings. Whether you sit inside or on the outdoor terrace, you won't be disappointed.

Kolachi Restaurant's Menu

At Kolachi Restaurant Ocean Mall, the question isn't whether to eat, but what to eat, given the sheer range of options. From traditional Pakistani flavors to international dishes, there's something for everyone.

Starters: A Flavorful Beginning

Choose from appetizers highly preferred by customers when dining at Kolachi — from crispy chicken wings to spicy prawn tempura, building anticipation for the meal ahead.

Soups & Salads

For something lighter to begin with, try the self-made chicken corn soup or the creamy mushroom soup, or a Caesar or Greek salad — especially good when it's chilly outside.

Burger Entrées

Not a regular burger joint's hamburgers — beef, chicken, or veggie burgers are juicy, buns are crisp, and there are plenty of optional toppings, sauces, and seasonings on the premium options.

Sandwiches

From club sandwiches to grilled paninis, these are filling meals served with crisp fries or a side salad — good for lunch or a lighter dinner.

Italian Cuisine

Craving pasta or pizza? Kolachi's Italian section impresses with Alfredo pasta, lasagna, and pizzas baked in an authentic wood-fired oven, using fresh ingredients and traditional recipes.

Beef and Chicken Steaks

Kolachi is among the best for steaks — juicy beef or well-done chicken steak, prepared with tenderness and served with accompanying sauces and garnishes, cooked to your desired taste.

Oriental Delights

For Asian food lovers — sweet and sour chicken, fried rice, and beef in black pepper, prepared with local and oriental spices for an Asian feel with a local twist.

Rice Dishes

Chicken biryani and prawn fried rice, well cooked and fused with aroma and spices, suitable alongside BBQ, curry, and oriental dishes.

Seafood

Fish, prawns, and calamari, marinated and grilled to perfection — grilled or fried, always fresh and tasty.

Desi Delicacies

Sajji, Matka Handi, and Katakat, cooked with real spices and traditional methods — including the popular desi karahi.

BBQ Temptations

Tender chicken tikka, juicy seekh kebab, and a whole range of marinated and grilled meats with smoky, charcoal-like tastes.

Hot Tandoor

Fresh tandoor bread — from naan to paratha — cooked in a clay oven, complementing curries, BBQ, and stews.

Refreshing Beverages

Cold drinks, milkshakes, smoothies, and mocktail specials to pair with your ordered meal.

Warm Comfort

Teas, strong coffees, and more exotic options such as Kashmiri chai — well suited after a heavy meal.

Desserts

Bellasimo ice creams — chocolate, vanilla, and other flavors to complete the meal.

Why Kolachi Restaurant Ocean Mall is Unique

What differentiates Kolachi Restaurant Ocean Mall is the type and kind of food offered alongside the feel of the restaurant. Strong and efficient service, a pleasant ambiance, and a variety of food to meet the demands of many guests make it one of the most popular restaurants in Karachi.

Conclusion

If you're in Karachi and searching for a restaurant that offers many types of well-prepared food in a good environment, Kolachi Restaurant Ocean Mall is for you. It offers a combination of foods and is well known for its quality service, attracting food lovers.
