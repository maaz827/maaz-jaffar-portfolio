About Me

Content that's written to rank — and written to be read.

A closer look at how I approach SEO content writing, and the practical experience behind it.

I'm Maaz Ansari, an SEO content writer and SEO specialist working across WordPress, keyword research, and search-focused article writing.

My approach starts with the reader's search intent, not the keyword count. I research what people are actually looking for, look at what's already ranking, and build content around that — clear headings, useful structure, and answers placed where a reader (and a search engine) expects them.

Over the past roughly two years, I've worked hands-on with WordPress and Google AdSense-monetized sites, writing and publishing SEO-focused blog and article content. That includes formatting articles for readability, structuring headings (H1/H2/H3), adding internal links, and implementing on-page SEO directly in WordPress — not just handing off a Word document and hoping for the best.

I've written content that's reached Google's first page through on-page SEO and keyword-focused structuring. I don't publish specific ranking numbers or traffic figures here unless I can back them with evidence — what you'll find on this site instead is the actual writing and structure behind that work, so you can judge the craft directly.

Outside of the writing itself, I care about the practical mechanics of SEO: search intent analysis, SERP research, internal and external linking, meta title and description optimization, and content gap analysis against competing pages. I use tools like Ahrefs and Google Search alongside AI-assisted writing and plagiarism-checking tools as part of a normal editorial workflow — not as a replacement for research or original writing.

Before focusing on SEO content, I spent time in customer-facing and process-driven roles — cold calling, lead generation, and loan recovery — which is where a lot of my communication, problem-solving, and critical-thinking habits come from. See the Experience page for the full picture.
