Restaurant Lahore

Ghani Shinwari Restaurant

Structuring a large, multi-category traditional restaurant menu into a single, scannable article matched to local search intent.

Objective

Present the full, multi-category Ghani Shinwari menu (Karahi, Rosh, Pulao, BBQ, Kabab) as a single organized article that a local searcher could use to decide what to order, while giving the restaurant's three Lahore branches clear representation.

Target Keyword

Ghani Shinwari Restaurant Lahore (menu, prices, karahi, pulao) — inferred from the article's title and heading structure, not from external keyword-tool data.

Search Intent

Primarily informational-local: someone searching for this restaurant's menu, prices, or specific dishes (e.g. Shinwari Karahi, Khada Dumba) before or during a visit — with a secondary transactional angle around home delivery.

Research

The article breaks the menu into its natural sub-categories (Karahi, Rosh, Pulao, BBQ, Kabab, Roti & Extras) rather than treating it as one undifferentiated list — mirroring how a diner would actually browse a physical menu, and how a search engine would want the content chunked under distinct H2s.

Content Strategy

A single H1 covers the restaurant and location; each menu category gets its own H2; a dedicated 'Dish Recommendations' section re-surfaces the most-searched items in a scannable, numbered format; a closing FAQ block answers the practical questions a local searcher is most likely to have (spice level, vegetarian options, delivery, price range).

On-Page SEO

Real H1/H2 heading hierarchy was already present in the original document — the strongest structural foundation of any article in this portfolio. For the web version, IDs were added to every heading for a working table of contents, and pricing was kept in scannable list form rather than buried in paragraphs.

Publishing

Formatted for WordPress-style publishing: heading hierarchy, internal linking to related articles, and an FAQ block structured so it could be marked up with FAQ schema if published live.

Result

Performance data not publicly disclosed. No ranking position, traffic, or revenue figures are claimed for this piece — this case study documents process and structure only.
