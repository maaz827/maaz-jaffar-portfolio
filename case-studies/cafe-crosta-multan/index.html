Cafe Multan

Cafe Crosta Multan

Organizing an unusually broad 15-category menu into content a reader can actually navigate without getting lost.

Objective

Turn a genuinely large 15-category menu (appetizers through smoothies and desserts) into content a reader could actually navigate on a page, rather than a wall of undifferentiated text.

Target Keyword

Cafe Crosta Multan menu, details, prices — inferred from the article's title, matching how a local searcher would phrase the query.

Search Intent

Local informational intent from someone deciding whether to visit — wanting a sense of the full menu breadth (European, Italian, Asian, desi, and dessert sections) before choosing where to eat.

Research

The original content already segmented the menu into 15 numbered categories. The main research task was recognizing that 15 flat sections would overwhelm a reader online, and that the content needed a clear top-level 'Menu' anchor with each category demoted to H3 under it, so the table of contents stays usable.

Content Strategy

Atmosphere and location context lead the article (matching intent for someone deciding where to dine), followed by the full category-by-category menu, and closing with a 'why different' section that gives the piece a differentiated angle rather than reading as a plain menu list.

On-Page SEO

Restructured from flat, unstyled paragraphs into a proper H1 → H2 → H3 hierarchy for the web version, since the source document used no Word heading styles at all. A working table of contents was added to help readers jump directly to a menu category.

Publishing

Formatted for WordPress-style publishing with internal links to related local-restaurant articles and a 5-question FAQ block covering reservations, delivery, and dietary options.
