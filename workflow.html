Workflow

A repeatable, search-first content process.

Nine steps, from initial keyword research through to published, optimized content in WordPress.

01

Keyword Research

Research relevant keywords using tools such as Ahrefs and Google, looking for terms that match real search demand and reasonable relevance to the topic.

02

Search Intent

Understand what the searcher is actually looking for — informational, transactional, or navigational — and what type of content should satisfy that query.

03

SERP Research

Analyze search results and competing pages to understand the content currently ranking, its structure, and where there's room to do better.

04

Content Planning

Create a logical structure using H1, H2, and H3 headings before writing a single sentence of body content.

05

Content Writing

Write useful, readable content around the target keyword and search intent — prioritizing clarity over keyword stuffing.

06

On-Page SEO

Optimize headings, keyword placement, metadata, links, and structure, along with other relevant on-page elements.

07

Content Review

Check the content for readability, originality, grammar, and SEO requirements before it goes anywhere near publish.

08

WordPress Publishing

Publish and format the article directly in WordPress — headings, images, internal links, and layout included.

09

Optimization

Monitor performance where data is available and improve content when necessary.
